A GIF of the area you leave open.

The toolbar stays on screen. Next to it is a clear window. Only that piece is recorded, straight to a GIF.

Just the rectangle

No full-screen recording and no hiding in the tray. The window stays where you put it.

Area

Resize the window. Recording takes the pixels next to the toolbar.

GIF

Frames are written as a GIF. A per-frame palette and rectangle diffs keep the file smaller.

File

The finished GIF is saved in your Pictures folder.
